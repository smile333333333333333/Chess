🌎 The Worlds

Each rival changes the board colors and plays according to a different strategic preference. Special tile patterns are decorative in this version; the chess rules remain the same.

How to play

1. You play White and move first.

2. Tap a white piece. Green dots show available moves. A red ring marks a capture.

3. Tap a destination square. The computer responds automatically.

4. Reach checkmate to win. If the current player has no legal moves and isn't in check, the game is a draw.

This prototype includes standard piece movement, check, checkmate, stalemate, and automatic queen promotion. Castling and en passant are not implemented. The computer is a lightweight custom AI, not a professional chess engine.
